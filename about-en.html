About Me

I specialise in business process automation — from complex Excel workbooks and Google Sheets to Power BI, web applications, Telegram bots, integrations, and analytical solutions for business.

Not just code.
A working system.

What I Actually Do

My job is not simply to write a formula or a script. First, I examine how the process works, where errors occur, what people do manually, and what result they actually need.

Then I build a solution that removes unnecessary actions, reduces errors, and makes the process clearer for the people who use it.

I do not automate things for the sake of automation. If a task can be solved with a sensible formula, I will not build a spaceship for it.

How It All Started

When I first saw a computer in the early 2000s, I had no idea what Excel, formulas, macros, or automation were. I didn't even know that, to calculate something as simple as two plus two, a formula had to start with an equals sign.

What followed were months and years of practice, hundreds of mistakes, hundreds of thousands of lines of code, and a constant desire to understand how things really work.

Over time, I realized that time is the most valuable resource. There is no point in endlessly copying data, checking the same numbers repeatedly, moving information between spreadsheets, or spending hours on routine tasks if a system can do the same work in seconds.

That is exactly why I enjoy automation. I like finding repetitive processes, simplifying them, and turning hours of monotonous work into just a few seconds of efficient execution.

It all started with one simple question: why doesn't two plus two work without an equals sign?
