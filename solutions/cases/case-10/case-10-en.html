REAL-WORLD CASE #10

Generate contracts automatically from an approved template

Client information, billing details, and commercial terms are entered once. The system inserts them into the approved contract template, applies the selected formatting, and prepares the document for printing or PDF export.

PAIN POINT

Every new contract started with a copy of the last one

A manager opens a previous client's contract, makes a copy, and manually replaces the company name, contact person, legal details, bank information, dates, pricing, and commercial terms.

  • the same information has to be updated in multiple places throughout the document;
  • every reference to the previous client has to be found and replaced manually;
  • one missed company name, address, or IBAN can end up in another client's contract;
  • every change requires another full review of the document;
  • different employees may format the same type of contract differently;
  • preparing a standard agreement takes time even though most of the document never changes.
SOLUTION

One approved template. A new contract in minutes.

The company's approved contract template is configured once. From then on, the user works only with a simple data-entry form, while the complete contract is generated automatically.

  • client details are entered once and inserted everywhere they are needed;
  • pricing, deposits, deadlines, and other terms automatically flow into the correct clauses;
  • the template keeps the company's approved structure and wording intact;
  • fonts, margins, headers, footers, page numbers, and borders are controlled through settings;
  • formatting changes appear instantly in the live preview;
  • the finished agreement can be printed or saved as a PDF.
