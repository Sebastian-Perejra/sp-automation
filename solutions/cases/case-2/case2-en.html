Real case study #2

Automated parsing of PDF invoices and packing lists

PDF files are uploaded to an incoming Google Drive folder. After launch, the script performs OCR, identifies the document type, parses line items, writes data to the RAW and PACKING sheets, matches weights by a composite key, and moves each processed file to the archive. All product names, article numbers, quantities and prices below are fictional.
