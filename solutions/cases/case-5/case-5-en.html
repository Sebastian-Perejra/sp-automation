Real case study #5

Automated component requirements calculation

The sales department creates orders for finished machines and production equipment. Each product has its own bill of materials (BOM), containing component codes and the required quantity per unit. The system converts finished-product orders into total component requirements, compares them with current warehouse stock and calculates the exact quantity that needs to be purchased.

Problem

Manual calculations take too much time

A purchasing specialist has to open multiple specifications, multiply component quantities by the number of ordered machines, consolidate identical parts and compare the result with warehouse stock. This creates a risk of calculation errors, shortages, excessive purchases and production delays.

Solution

Automated purchasing planning

The user enters the number of ordered machines and the available stock. The system expands each product BOM, consolidates requirements by component code and calculates the exact shortage that must be purchased.
