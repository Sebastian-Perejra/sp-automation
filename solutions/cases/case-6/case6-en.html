REAL CASE STUDY #6

Management Reporting Automation in Power BI

The company manufactures and sells juices, mineral water, lemonades, iced tea and energy drinks. Sales data is stored in an accounting system such as 1C, SAP or another ERP platform. Previously, an employee manually exported the data to Excel every month, added it to the existing file, checked formulas, refreshed pivot tables and prepared a new report version for management.

PROBLEM

The management report became outdated before it was even sent

Data was manually transferred from the accounting system into Excel. Every update required a significant amount of time, depended on one specific employee and created a risk of errors in formulas, ranges, pivot tables and charts.

  • multiple versions of the same report;
  • manual copying of new data;
  • delayed management information;
  • no ability to quickly change the analytical view;
  • dependence on the employee responsible for the Excel file.
SOLUTION

Automatic model refresh and an interactive Power BI report

Power BI connects to the source through a connector, API, ODBC connection or directly to the database. If the system is located inside the company network, an On-premises Data Gateway is used.

  • automatic scheduled data refresh;
  • one shared model for all users;
  • filters, slicers and cross-filtering;
  • plan-versus-actual analysis and thresholds;
  • daily access to up-to-date indicators for management.
