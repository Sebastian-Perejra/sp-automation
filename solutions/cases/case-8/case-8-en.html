Real case study #8

Automating reminders and task follow-up with a Telegram bot

Users create reminders directly in Telegram: enter a task, choose when it should come back, select the reminder mode, and let the bot bring it back at the right moment. Important messages, follow-ups and personal tasks no longer depend on memory or disappear in busy chat histories.

The pain

Messages, tasks and commitments get buried in chat history

An important message often arrives at the wrong moment. You read it, think “I’ll get back to this later”, move on to something else and eventually forget about it.

That is how client replies, follow-ups, calls, payments, personal notes and other small but important actions get missed.

Telegram may already contain all the information you need, but the conversation itself does not guarantee that the task will actually be completed.

The solution

The Telegram bot turns a message into a controlled reminder

The user opens the bot, taps “Add”, enters the task, chooses the date and time, and selects how the reminder should behave. The bot stores the task and brings it back automatically when needed.

A reminder can be completed, snoozed, repeated, reviewed in the list or passed to another person.

The entire workflow stays inside Telegram — without a separate task manager or another tool to maintain.
