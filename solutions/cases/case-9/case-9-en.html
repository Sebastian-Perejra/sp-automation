Real-World Case #9

From Customer Request to a Ready-to-Send Sales Quotation

A complete sales quotation workflow for a manufacturing company: from the customer email and the sales manager’s actual desktop to customer lookup, product and price sources, line items, delivery terms, PDF generation, and sending.

Problem

Sales quotations are often assembled manually from data scattered across multiple systems

The request arrives by email. Customer data may live in CRM, ERP, 1C, or Excel, while products and current pricing are stored elsewhere. The quotation itself is then built in Word, Excel, or an old template.

01 Risk of outdated pricing A sales manager may use an outdated price list or miss a customer-specific price.
02 Copy-and-paste errors SKUs, quantities, customer details, delivery addresses, and payment terms are transferred manually.
03 Stale data in copied templates A copied quotation may still contain another customer’s name, date, price, or terms.
04 Unclear discount handling It may be unclear which discounts a sales manager can apply and when approval is required.
05 Delivery is calculated separately Delivery cost, address, Incoterms, and lead time may sit outside the quotation workflow.
06 No single audit trail After a quotation is sent, it can be difficult to quickly see what was offered, when, and at what price.
Solution

One guided workflow connects the request, customer, products, pricing, delivery, and final document

After receiving the email, the sales manager opens a regular desktop shortcut called “Sales Quotations.” It is an internal company application that opens like any other work app and connects to the data sources the business already uses.

✓

The sales manager works in one clear interface instead of opening scripts or technical consoles.

✓

Every price has a clear source: ERP, 1C, CRM, Excel, or another database.

✓

Customer details are pulled from the customer master.

✓

Quantities, discounts, delivery, and commercial terms are managed in one place.

✓

The PDF is generated from current data instead of a copied legacy file.

✓

The finished quotation is stored in the “Sales Quotations” module while configured integrations run in the background.

Business value Less manual data transfer, fewer errors, and a much more transparent process from customer request to sent PDF.
